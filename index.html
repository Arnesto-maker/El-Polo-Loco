EL POLLO LOCO – TACTICAL EDITION

Forget everything you know about standard jump-and-run games. In the scorching Mexican desert, Pepe faces the ultimate feathered nightmare!

Tactical Boss Combat: Break through a 100-point armor shield, aim for critical vertical hit-zones, and survive escalating bullet-hell salvo phases.

Smart Resource Economy: Collect coins and use the live in-game Coin Converter (Press B) to restock salsa bottles in the heat of battle.

Adaptive AI: Watch your step—three sneaky back-hits will trigger an instant turnaround combo!

Engineering & Architecture: Built from scratch using Pure Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and powered by Gemini 4 Argon. The Image Assets of this Game are Product of Developer Akademie GmbH
